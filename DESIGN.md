# Design system

## Operational hierarchy

- **Base / quiet:** `#0A0810` and `#1B1426` keep large surfaces calm.
- **Structure:** armor purple frames regions without competing with live data.
- **Live / selected:** signal green is the sharpest luminance jump in the system.
- **Attention:** electric orange marks warnings, hotkeys, and transitional state.
- **Failure:** alarm pink-red remains distinct from orange so destructive actions read.
- **Data families:** cyan and periwinkle separate secondary graph channels.

## Contrast intent

Warm paper foreground on the terminal base is approximately 17:1. Signal green on the
terminal base is approximately 15:1. Muted text is reserved for secondary metadata; it
should not be used for essential small copy.

## Wallpaper composition

All three images preserve a darker left field for terminals and launchers. Bright focal
geometry lives on the right or at the central vanishing point, so tiled windows stay
legible without demanding a blur layer.

## Naming

`SYNC-01` is an original theme-system label, not an official franchise mark. File names
describe the scene rather than copying in-world organization or interface terminology.
